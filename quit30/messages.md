# Quit in 30 Days: messages for TikTok and Instagram

Replace `[NAME]` with the person's name and `[LINK]` with your sales link before sending.

**Golden rules**
- Message only people who are already interested: they commented on your video, liked a post about quitting, follow quit-smoking pages, or wrote that they want to quit.
- Never judge, shame or lecture. Many smokers have tried before and feel bad about it. The tone is: "I get it, here is something that helps."
- No promises like "you will quit for sure" or "guaranteed". Say what the book gives: a page a day, a plan for cravings, a Save Box.
- Do not put the link in the first message. Start a conversation, send the link when they say yes.
- Keep to about 10–20 messages a day, so your account is not limited.
