Welcome

You can do this.

You have probably tried before, and you are still here. That says a lot about you.

This book gives you one page for each of your 30 smoke-free days. Every day gives you a paper task, a real-life task and a surprising fact. Do one page a day and let the days add up.

Remember, change comes from within…

Every day you do not smoke is a day you give back to yourself.

QUIT IN 30 DAYS4
Your toolkit

Three tools you will use every day

1. Save Box. Each day, put the money a pack would have cost into a jar you can see.
2. Store Question. Heading out to buy cigarettes? Ask: why go to the store to buy cigarettes, when I can buy something else instead? Then buy a drink, a snack or gum.
3. Full Breath. Three slow breaths morning and evening. Notice how your breathing changes.

When a craving hits (it lasts only a few minutes)

  1. Name it: “This is a craving. It will pass.”
  2. Take three Full Breaths.
  3. Drink water in slow sips.
  4. Move: stand up, step outside, change rooms.
  5. Keep your hands and mouth busy: gum, a snack, a pen.
  6. Read your reasons card and picture your sport.

General information, not medical advice. If you have a health condition, are pregnant, take medication or want nicotine replacement or prescription help, talk to a doctor or pharmacist first.

QUIT IN 30 DAYS6
Before Day 1

Get ready

  1. Write your top 3 reasons on a card and on your phone. Be specific, like “I want to play football without losing my breath.”
  2. Name your sport or activity. Put it at the top of the card. You will test it on Day 30.
  3. Pick a quit date within the next 7 to 14 days, and tell one person.
  4. Build your Save Box. Use a clear jar. Daily save = price of a pack × packs per day. Write it on the jar and choose what the money is for.
  5. Note your top 3 triggers (coffee, stress, drinks, driving) and decide what you will do instead.
  6. On the night before: throw away all cigarettes, lighters and ashtrays, and stock gum, water and snacks.
A quit date you have told someone about is a promise, not a wish.

Before Day 1

Choose your method

The best method is the one you will follow. Pairing a method with support usually works better than going alone.

MethodGood if
Cold turkeyYou want a clean break and have a plan for cravings
Cutting down, then stoppingStopping suddenly feels overwhelming (set an end date)
Nicotine replacement (patch, gum, lozenge)Cravings are strong. Follow the package, ask a pharmacist
Prescription help or a quitlineYou have tried before and want extra support
Talk to a doctor or pharmacist first if you are pregnant, have a heart or lung condition, take regular medication, are under 18, or have a history of depression or anxiety.

General reasons fade. Specific ones keep you going on the hard days.

Your Save Box, in one line

price of a pack × packs per day = your daily save

Example only: if a pack costs 5 and you smoke 1 pack a day, that is 5 a day, 35 after one week and 150 after 30 days. Your own numbers may be bigger.

If it happens

If you slip

One cigarette is a moment, not a verdict. Prepare for a bad day, so it does not become a bad week.

  1. Put it out.
  2. Take three Full Breaths.
  3. Tell someone.
  4. Go back on the plan, today, at the page you were on.
Fewer than 4 in 10 adults who try to quit use counselling or medication. If you slip, adding support is one of the best ways to improve your next try.
Quitting is not about never falling. It is about always getting back up.
